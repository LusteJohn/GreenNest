Our story

Rooted in the everyday joy of green.

GreenNest began with a few pots on a Sagay windowsill and a simple belief: the right plant can make a room feel more like home. Today, we grow and gather resilient favorites for first-time plant parents and seasoned collectors alike.

Plant care, made simple

A greener thumb starts with a good plan.

You do not need perfect conditions or a long checklist. Notice a little, adjust gently, and let your plant show you the way.

Ask us about a plant

Start with light.

Watch how the sun moves through your space, then choose a plant that fits the brightness you actually have.

Water with intention.

Check the soil before you reach for the can. Most plants prefer a thoughtful drink to a fixed schedule.

Give it time.

New leaves and small changes are progress. A plant is a relationship, not a one-day project.

Find your next companion in person.
